Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016

13. Customer Due Diligence Procedure
13.1 REs shall undertake CDD measures while:
(i) commencing an account-based relationship;
(ii) carrying out a transaction of Rs. 1,00,000 and above (whether conducted as a single transaction or several transactions that appear to be connected) for persons not holding an account with the RE;
(iii) carrying out any international money transfer operations;
(iv) when the RE has doubts about the veracity or adequacy of previously obtained customer identification data.

13.2 REs shall obtain the following information/documents for the purpose of CDD:
(i) For Individuals: an officially valid document (OVD) for proof of identity and proof of address.
(ii) For Legal Entities: Certificate of incorporation/registration, Memorandum and Articles of Association, Board resolution authorising officials, and identification documents of beneficial owners holding more than 15% shares.

22. Enhanced Due Diligence
22.1 REs shall carry out Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for higher risk customers, including:
(a) Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their family members and close associates;
(b) Non-Resident Customers;
(c) High Net Worth Individuals.

22.2 The REs shall:
(i) obtain senior management approval for establishing business relationships with PEPs;
(ii) conduct enhanced ongoing monitoring of the business relationship.

38. Risk Categorisation and Monitoring
38.1 REs shall put in place a risk-based approach for management of risks. All the accounts opened shall be categorised under two risk categories: Low and High.

46. Record Management
46.2 The records referred to in paragraph 46.1 shall be maintained for a period of three years from the date of the transactions between the RE and the customer. Such records shall be made available on request to the competent authorities.
