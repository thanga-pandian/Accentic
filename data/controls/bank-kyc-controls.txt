MERIDIAN BANK LIMITED
Internal Control Framework — Customer Onboarding, AML & KYC
Document Ref: MBL-ICF-KYC-v4.2
Owner: Chief Compliance Officer
Last Review: 2025-11-30

SECTION A — CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE

CTRL-KYC-001 | Identity Verification at Onboarding
Description: All new account-based relationships require collection and system
validation of one Officially Valid Document (OVD) for proof of identity and proof
of address before the account is activated. The onboarding platform blocks
activation until the OVD check returns a pass.
Owner: Head of Onboarding Operations
Frequency: Per transaction (real-time)
Type: Preventive
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-10-14
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: OVD validation logs, Onboarding audit trail ONB-LOG-2025

CTRL-KYC-002 | Legal Entity Documentation Pack
Description: For non-individual customers the relationship manager must obtain
certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, and the
board resolution authorising signatories. Documents are indexed in the Entity
Document Repository.
Owner: Corporate Banking Operations
Frequency: Per onboarding
Type: Preventive
Automated: No
Last Tested: 2025-09-02
Test Result: Partially Effective
Evidence: Entity Document Repository index, sample pack review Q3-2025

CTRL-KYC-003 | Beneficial Ownership Identification
Description: Where the customer is a company, ownership is traced and any natural
person holding more than 25 percent is recorded as a beneficial owner in the
customer master. Identity of each beneficial owner is verified to the same
standard as an individual customer.
Owner: Financial Crime Unit
Frequency: Per onboarding and on ownership change
Type: Preventive
Automated: Partially
Last Tested: 2025-08-19
Test Result: Partially Effective
Evidence: Beneficial ownership register, UBO exception report

CTRL-KYC-004 | Occasional Transaction Threshold Check
Description: Walk-in and non-account-holder transactions are screened against a
value threshold. Transactions at or above the regulatory threshold trigger
mandatory customer identification before processing.
Owner: Branch Operations
Frequency: Per transaction (real-time)
Type: Preventive
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-10-14
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Teller system threshold config, TXN-THRESH-2025 report

SECTION B — RISK CATEGORISATION

CTRL-RISK-010 | Customer Risk Scoring Engine
Description: Every customer is assigned a Low, Medium or High risk category at
onboarding by the automated risk scoring engine using geography, product,
occupation and transaction profile inputs.
Owner: Financial Crime Unit
Frequency: At onboarding
Type: Detective
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-10-01
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Risk engine configuration, scoring distribution report RISK-Q4

CTRL-RISK-011 | Periodic Risk Review
Description: Customer risk classification is re-evaluated on a fixed cycle. High
risk customers are reviewed annually, medium risk every two years, low risk every
three years. Reviews are tracked to completion in the compliance workflow tool.
Owner: Financial Crime Unit
Frequency: Annual / biennial / triennial by tier
Type: Detective
Automated: Partially
Last Tested: 2025-07-22
Test Result: Partially Effective
Evidence: Periodic review completion dashboard — 312 of 4,180 high-risk reviews overdue

SECTION C — ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE

CTRL-EDD-020 | PEP Screening
Description: All customers and beneficial owners are screened against a licensed
politically exposed persons database at onboarding and rescreened nightly against
list updates. Matches raise a case in the alert management system.
Owner: Financial Crime Unit
Frequency: At onboarding and nightly
Type: Detective
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-10-28
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Screening vendor logs, nightly batch completion records

CTRL-EDD-021 | Senior Management Approval for High Risk Relationships
Description: Establishing or continuing a relationship with a politically exposed
person requires documented approval from the designated senior management
approver before the account can transact.
Owner: Chief Compliance Officer
Frequency: Per PEP relationship
Type: Preventive
Automated: No
Last Tested: 2025-06-11
Test Result: Needs Improvement
Evidence: PEP approval register — incomplete for 14 of 61 sampled relationships

SECTION D — RECORD MANAGEMENT

CTRL-REC-030 | Transaction Record Retention
Description: Records of all customer transactions are retained in the archival
platform for the regulatory minimum retention period from the date of the
transaction and are retrievable on request by competent authorities.
Owner: Head of Operations
Frequency: Continuous
Type: Preventive
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-09-30
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Archive retention policy config, retrieval test ARC-TEST-0925

CTRL-REC-031 | Transaction Reconstruction Capability
Description: The archival platform supports reconstruction of individual
transactions including counterparty, value, channel and timestamp to support
evidentiary requests.
Owner: Head of Operations
Frequency: Continuous
Type: Detective
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-09-30
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Reconstruction test results, sample case pack

SECTION E — REGULATORY REPORTING

CTRL-RPT-040 | Cash Transaction Reporting
Description: Cash transactions above the regulatory threshold are aggregated and
submitted to the financial intelligence unit on the prescribed monthly cycle via
the automated reporting gateway.
Owner: Regulatory Reporting Team
Frequency: Monthly
Type: Detective
Automated: Yes
Last Tested: 2025-10-15
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Monthly CTR submission acknowledgements — 1 of 12 submissions filed after the 15th-day deadline

CTRL-RPT-041 | Suspicious Transaction Reporting
Description: Alerts confirmed as suspicious by an investigator are escalated and
filed with the financial intelligence unit. Filing is tracked from conclusion of
investigation to submission.
Owner: Financial Crime Unit
Frequency: Per case
Type: Detective
Automated: Partially
Last Tested: 2025-08-05
Test Result: Needs Improvement
Evidence: STR case log — mean filing lag 11.4 days against 7 day requirement

CTRL-GOV-050 | Principal Officer Designation
Description: A Principal Officer is formally designated and their name,
designation and address are communicated to the financial intelligence unit. The
designation record is reviewed when the postholder changes.
Owner: Company Secretary
Frequency: On appointment and on change
Type: Preventive
Automated: No
Last Tested: 2025-05-20
Test Result: Effective
Evidence: Board minute of designation, FIU acknowledgement letter
